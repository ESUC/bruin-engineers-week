Our Story

Bruin Engineers Week (E-Week) is an annual week-long series of events hosted by ESUC during Week 3 of Spring Quarter in celebration of all things engineering.

Our Plan

Numerous events involving volunteering, networking, problem-solving, exercising, expressing ideas, and having fun will be available for students to attend.

Our Vision

Events held during Bruin E-Week are designed to bring students together through innovation, outreach, and fun as well as promote individual development.

Face the Faculty

At Face the Faculty, teams composed of students, staff and faculty face off in friendly games of dodgeball. Enjoy light refreshments and lots of fun!

  • Date: Thursday, January 15, 2026
  • Time: Dodgeball 1:00 PM - 3:00 PM
  • Location: IM Field

Join us in a battle of brawn and fight against faculty and student organizations.

Engineers' Talent Show

Engineers' Talent Show is organized to give engineering students the scene to showcase their amazing talents!

  • Date: Wednesday, January 14, 2026
  • Time: 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM
  • Location: Mong Auditorium (Engineering VI)

Are you tired of people thinking engineers are only good at math and science? It's time to prove them wrong! Come show off your hidden talents - whether that's singing, dancing, comedy, juggling, or something totally unique - at The Engineers' Talent Show!

Alumni Networking Night

ESUC's Alumni Night during Bruin Engineers Week, featuring UCLA engineering alumni and representatives from various student organizations coming together to share experiences and connect with current students.

  • Date: Tuesday, January 13, 2026

Get ready for Alumni Night during Bruin Engineers Week, as ESUC brings together alumni from engineering orgs for an evening full of stories, networking, and strong Bruin spirit.

Makers Day

Maker Day is a two-hour build-a-thon where students think outside the box to design and build creative solutions to a surprise prompt. It's a fun, hands-on challenge that encourages meeting new people, collaborating, and trying something new.

  • Date: Monday, January 12, 2026
  • Time: 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM
  • Location: Boelter Makerspace (1805 Boelter Hall)

Come to Makersday, where you will design and build a solution for a given prompt in teams of three! Creations will be judged by a panel, and cash prizes will be awarded to the top three teams.

Experience Engineering

Experience Engineering has UCLA Engineering student organizations create and present interactive workshops for local middle schoolers to learn about and become inspired by engineering.

  • Date: Friday, January 16, 2026
  • Time: 9:30 AM - 12:00 PM
  • Location: Engineering IV Patio

Bruin Engineers Week is a decades-old tradition at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) hosted by the Engineering Society of UCLA. During this week-long festivities, the UCLA engineering community gathers to celebrate the achievements of the engineering clubs and works together to give back to the greater community. One of the primary events is Experience Engineering, during which hundreds of elementary school students are invited to UCLA for a day of hands-on learning outside of a classroom setting. This is a rewarding experience for older students to volunteer their time to the community and help foster a love for learning and engineering in younger students.
